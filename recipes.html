My Favorite Bakes

Soft, cozy treats that bring warmth and sweetness to any day.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 ½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ cup softened butter
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 tbsp vanilla

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Whisk dry ingredients.
  3. Add butter, eggs, milk, and vanilla.
  4. Mix until smooth.
  5. Fill cupcake liners ¾ full.
  6. Bake 18–20 minutes.
